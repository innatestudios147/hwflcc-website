About the company

The Hopefield Wind Farm Local Community Company is a non-profit Section 21 association. We fund development for previously disadvantaged communities within 50 km of the Umoya Energy – Hopefield Wind Farm.

At least 85% of any expenditure benefits individuals classified as African, Indian or Coloured South African citizens, and all of our work takes place in South Africa.

What we fund

  • Education

    We fund four areas. In education: scholarships and bursaries, adult basic education and training, after-school programmes, support for teaching and school governance, and school infrastructure.

  • Social welfare

    Support for the elderly, for people who have been physically or mentally abused, and for persons with disabilities. The empowerment of women, help for children with special needs, and community infrastructure.

  • Health

    Healthcare programmes and initiatives that improve access to quality healthcare for the communities around the wind farm.

  • Development

    Training and support for emerging small businesses, skills and leadership development for young people, and local sport and cultural clubs.
