Where it
goes

Four areas, chosen for the communities around the wind farm.

Education

  • Scholarships and bursaries
  • Adult Basic Education & Training
  • After-school programmes
  • Teaching, curriculum and governance support
  • Infrastructure at schools

Social welfare

  • The elderly, the abused and people with disabilities
  • Empowerment and development of women
  • Children with special needs
  • Community infrastructure

Health

  • Healthcare programmes
  • Health initiatives
  • Better access to quality healthcare

Development

  • Training and support for small businesses
  • Self-help and skills projects
  • Skills and leadership for young people
  • Local sport and cultural clubs

  1. Check

    Make sure your project is in education, social welfare, health or development, and serves the Saldanha Bay Local Municipality.

  2. Prepare

    Complete the application form, write a proposal and attach your registration documents, a funding request letter and quotations.

  3. Send

    Post or deliver it to 26 Voortrekker Road, Hopefield, 7355.
