Who can apply

  • {{icon:check}}Registered NGOs
  • {{icon:check}}Schools
  • {{icon:check}}Early Childhood Development (ECD) centres
  • {{icon:check}}Local individuals
  • {{icon:check}}Other organisations

Applicants must provide community upliftment services in the Saldanha Bay Local Municipality. Funding supports four sectors: education, social welfare, health and development.

Selection criteria

  • Each application is assessed on its own merits.
  • Both existing projects and new or planned projects may apply.
  • Applications must be in writing and describe the project in full.
  • Registration documents and a letter requesting funding must accompany the application.
  • Students must include a quotation from their college or institution for registration fees or textbooks.
  • Schools and ECD centres need a letter requesting funding, approved by their Governing Body.

Applications that do not meet these requirements will not be considered. The HWFLCC’s decision is final and no correspondence will be entered into after adjudication.

What to submit

Your application pack

Submit the formal application form with supporting documents, together with a written proposal. Visual material — photographs, newspaper clippings, letters or illustrations — is encouraged.

Attachment checklist

  • {{icon:file}}Constitution
  • {{icon:file}}Registration certificate
  • {{icon:file}}List of management committee details
  • {{icon:file}}Governing Body letter (schools and ECD centres)
  • {{icon:file}}Quotations (students: from your college or institution)
  • {{icon:file}}Other information, such as photos

The form asks for

  • Contact details, registration status (NGO / PBO number), banking details and your accountant, bookkeeper or auditor.
  • Your organisation’s background, aims, services and beneficiaries.
  • The project: the need it addresses, number of beneficiaries, intervention, benefits, location and timeframe.
  • An itemised budget, and how and when you will monitor and evaluate the project.
  • Partnerships, and any other funding you have applied for.
  • A declaration signed by the applicant and the chairperson.
