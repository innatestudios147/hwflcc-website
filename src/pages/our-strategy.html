Our strategy

Investing for the long term.

How the Hopefield Wind Farm Local Community Company decides where support goes — and what it cannot fund.

Our mandate

Objects of the Company

To carry on, in a non-profit manner and with an altruistic and philanthropic intent, socio-economic development programmes for the benefit of the public in general — with the specific objective of facilitating sustainable access to the economy by previously disadvantaged communities residing within a 50 km radius of the Hopefield Wind Farm — provided that:

  1. a

    at all times, at least 85% of any expenditure by the Company shall be for the benefit of individuals classified as African, Indian or Coloured persons who are South African citizens by birth or descent, or are citizens of the Republic of South Africa by naturalisation occurring before 1993, or occurring after 1993 but who would have been naturalised prior to 1993 but for the Apartheid policy; and

  2. b

    all of the activities of the Company shall be based in the Republic of South Africa.

Funding principle

Most of every year’s budget goes to projects that last.

80% of the Local Company’s budget each year is spent on long-term sustainable projects, with the remaining 20% allocated to smaller projects.

Exclusions

What we will not fund

The following activities are excluded from funding by the Hopefield Wind Farm Local Community Company (HWFLCC).

  • {{icon:ban}}Political parties.
  • {{icon:ban}}Trade unions.
  • {{icon:ban}}Individual attendance of national and international conferences and trips.
  • {{icon:ban}}Activities or organisations whose objectives infringe the Bill of Rights of the Constitution or the legislation of the Republic of South Africa.
  • {{icon:ban}}Organisations or activities aimed at achieving the objectives of a particular religious group.
  • {{icon:ban}}Private companies and close corporations.
  • {{icon:ban}}Expenses that have already been incurred.
