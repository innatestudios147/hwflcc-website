About the wind farm

Umoya Energy – Hopefield Wind Farm

Located 125 km north of Cape Town on the R45 in the Saldanha Bay Municipality, Western Cape, the wind farm generates approximately 176 600 MWh of clean renewable energy every year.

It began supplying the grid in late 2013 and reached commercial operation on 1 February 2014 — just over a year after construction began, and the first commercial wind farm in South Africa to do so.

  • 176 600 MWh clean energy / year
  • 2014 Commercial operation
  • #1 First commercial wind farm in SA
