Privacy policy

How we handle personal information collected through this website.

Who we are

This website is operated by the Hopefield Wind Farm Local Community Company (HWFLCC), an association incorporated under Section 21, of 26 Voortrekker Road, Hopefield, 7355, Western Cape, South Africa. We process personal information in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

What we collect

We only collect personal information that you choose to give us. When you use the contact form we collect your name, email address, and — if you provide them — your phone number, organisation and the content of your message.

Funding applications sent to us by email or post are handled under the process described on the Apply for funding page.

How we use it

We use the information you send us only to respond to your enquiry and, where relevant, to consider a funding application. We do not sell or share your information for marketing.

Where it is stored

Contact form submissions are received and stored by our website host, Netlify, Inc., which processes the data on our behalf and may store it outside South Africa. We keep enquiries only as long as we need them to respond, or as required by law.

Cookies and third parties

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, and the map on our Contact page is provided by Google Maps; these services may receive your IP address and set their own cookies under Google’s privacy policy.

Your rights

You may ask us what personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. To do so, use our contact form or write to us at the address above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa.
